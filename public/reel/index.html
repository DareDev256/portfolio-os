ACT II

What carried over

The tooling is unrecognisable. The decisions are the same ones.

Same job

  • Deciding the frame before the shoot. A shot list is a shot list whether a crew or a model executes it.
  • Colour decided first. A ground and one accent, committed before anything is lit.
  • Cutting to the beat, and cutting the shot you love when it does not serve the run.
  • Delivering on a budget that was never generous.

Different camera

  • The crew is an API, so the bottleneck moved from scheduling to specification.
  • A bad take costs cents, so the discipline moved to not paying for the same bad take thirty times.
  • Nothing verifies itself. A generator will hand back a square frame having been asked for widescreen, and say nothing.
  • Volume is free; taste is not. Thirty pretty frames and zero ideas is now the default output.
