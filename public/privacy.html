Privacy

This site is operated by James Olusoga, an AI solutions engineer in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It exists to show work and to let you get in touch. It collects as little as it can and sells nothing.

What is collected

  • Usage analytics. Google Analytics 4 (property G-TG10CNCMJY) records anonymised page views and clicks. IP addresses are truncated by Google before storage, and advertising personalisation is off. This is used only to see which pages are read.
  • The "Let's talk" box. Free text you type there is sent to an AI model (Anthropic's API) to compose a reply. The text is not saved on this site's servers. A per-visitor cap of ten messages is enforced in memory and is discarded when the server restarts; no account and no cookie is created for it.
  • WhatsApp and email links. Tapping one opens WhatsApp or your mail app. Nothing about that tap is stored here.
  • Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address as any web request would.

What is not collected

No account, no newsletter, no advertising cookies, no third-party trackers beyond the analytics named above, no sale or sharing of data with anyone.

Your rights

Personal information is handled under PIPEDA (Canada). If you sent something through the site and want it deleted, or want to know what exists, use the WhatsApp link on the home page or the email in the résumé and it will be handled directly by James.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets its own first-party cookies to distinguish returning visits. This site sets none of its own.
